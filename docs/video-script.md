# MeaningSync Video Script

**Target runtime: 2:35**

**0:00–0:18 — Problem**  
“A customer and service provider can leave one verbal conversation believing different things—especially across languages. MeaningSync helps them check whether both sides mean the same thing.”

**0:18–0:35 — Homepage**  
Show the headline, agreement preview, English/Hindi capability, and trust promises. Select **Try the demo**.

**0:35–1:05 — Bilingual conversation**  
Choose **English / Hindi Demo**. Show the Homeowner’s original Hindi and Electrician’s original English, with translated views beneath—not replacing—the originals. Note that Demo is deterministic and key-free.

**1:05–1:30 — Agreement map**  
Show matching scope, ₹1,200 labour price, and today’s start. Highlight the materials conflict: one side included replacement parts; the other charged them separately. Show payment timing and warranty as not discussed.

**1:30–1:52 — Private resolution**  
Submit the Homeowner’s Hindi choice, show that it stays hidden, then hand off and submit the Electrician’s English choice. Explain that equivalent language options share one semantic ID.

**1:52–2:12 — Confirmation and receipt**  
Show each person confirming the same version separately, then open the bilingual clarity receipt with English/Hindi terms, unresolved/open sections, timestamps, evidence, and identity/legal disclaimers.

**2:12–2:27 — Technology**  
“GPT-5.6 performs structured agreement reasoning and meaning-preserving Live translation. OpenAI Realtime supplies reviewed transcripts. Codex accelerated the architecture, regression tests, and guided UI.”

**2:27–2:35 — Boundary**  
“MeaningSync preserves words and exposes differences. It does not verify identity, promise perfect translation, or create a legal contract.”
